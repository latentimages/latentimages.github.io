About Tal Lazar

Tal Lazar is a visual storyteller with over 20 years of experience as an award-winning filmmaker, 15 years as a professor at top universities, and a business leader at the intersection of technology, education, and entertainment.

As a cinematographer, producer, and director, Lazar’s work spans continents—from three box-office hits in Vietnam to more than a dozen feature films and hundreds of productions in the U.S.

In higher education, Lazar has led some of the most sought-after visual storytelling courses at institutions like the American Film Institute Conservatory, Columbia University, and the City College of New York. He’s a frequent guest lecturer worldwide, from the Yanjing Institute of Technology in China to the Sundance Institute in the U.S., and his online workshops are featured on Berklee Music Online, MZed.com, and more.An innovator from an early age, Lazar was honored with the Israeli President’s Award for his pioneering work in technology, later founding the EdTech startup Quonder and leading production studio MiLa Media.

Now, Lazar blends his expertise in filmmaking, education, and technology to advise businesses and entrepreneurs. His forthcoming book, The Language of Cinematography, is set for release in 2025.

The Cinematic Impact Framework™ draws inspiration from the way films are made, translating the director’s ability to shape a compelling vision into a powerful tool for businesses. Just as movie directors guide their teams to create immersive stories across images, sound, and text, this framework helps organizations deliver impactful messages that seamlessly translate across mediums, just like a movie does. Lazar goes beyond traditional video production, equipping businesses with the storytelling techniques used in cinema to create narratives that captivate audiences, foster deep engagement, and drive meaningful business results.

“When people talk about stories, they often miss the emotional core that makes movies so powerful.”says Lazar. "I created the Cinematic Impact Framework™ to change that, and to help organizations forge deep connections and drive real change, both within their teams and in the world.”

Lazar’s cinematic storytelling techniques blend the art of filmmaking with strategic business messaging to create a narrative that is not only compelling but transformational. By leveraging the three pillars of the framework—Visual Story, Director’s Vision, and CinematicLanguage—businesses can tap into the heart of their mission, amplify their impact, and craft stories that captivate their audience.
